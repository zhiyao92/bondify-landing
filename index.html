Made for everyday use

For the people you want to stay close to

A busy week can turn every conversation into logistics. Bondify gives you a starting point, whether you want a lighter question over dinner, a thoughtful check-in, or a calmer conversation after a misunderstanding.

Talk

Find a question worth asking

Choose a conversation deck or a suggestion for the person you have in mind. Answer together, or take turns when your schedules do not line up.

Connect

Check in, then reveal together

A five-minute, two-person check-in gives each of you space to answer privately. Responses are revealed when both people are ready.

Repair

Give a difficult moment some structure

Move through feelings, perspectives and needs, then choose one small next step. The aim is understanding, not deciding who was right.

Bonds

Keep each relationship in its own space

Create a Bond for a partner, parent, sibling, friend or another person in your family. Keep that relationship’s conversations, memories and reminders together.

Memories

Save more than the conversation

Add photos and captions, keep a shared wishlist and revisit the promises and moments you want to remember.

Optional sync

Connect across devices, deliberately

Use supported sync features when you want them. Pairing explicitly connects two people to a private Bond; there is no public posting or social feed.

A simple place to start

Start with one person.

  1. Choose one relationship

    Start with a Bond for someone you want to make more time for. You do not need to organize every relationship at once.

  2. Pick the right kind of conversation

    Use Talk for an open question, Connect for a brief check-in, or Repair when both of you want to work through everyday friction.

  3. Leave with one small next step

    Keep a memory or revisit a promise. A short conversation you return to is more useful than a routine that feels like homework.

Free and paid options

Start free. Grow at your own pace.

Free to download

Start with meaningful conversations, a daily check-in and a Bond. You can try the core rhythm before choosing whether to upgrade.

Optional Bondify Plus

Plus unlocks unlimited Bonds, the full conversation catalog and every guided plan. The US App Store currently lists a $1.99 weekly plan and a $59.99 yearly plan.

Plus is an auto-renewing subscription. Prices and available plans can vary by country; review the price and renewal terms shown in the app before subscribing. Your saved memories remain readable if you stop subscribing. US App Store information checked 6 November 2026.

Privacy and support

Bondify has no public feed. Its App Store description says conversation answers and Repair responses are not collected as analytics content. Optional sync and pairing mean it should not be treated as an entirely on-device-only app.

A few things before you connect.

Is Bondify only for couples?

No. It supports separate Bonds for partners, friends, parents, siblings and other family relationships.

Do we have to answer at the same time?

Talk can be used together or asynchronously. Connect keeps answers private until both people are ready for the shared reveal.

Does Bondify replace counseling?

No. It is a conversation and reflection tool, not therapy or an emergency service. Use Repair for everyday friction when both people are comfortable participating.

Can I start for free?

Yes. The free app includes meaningful conversations, a daily check-in and a Bond. Plus expands the catalog, guided plans and number of Bonds.

What happens to memories if I cancel Plus?

The current App Store description says your memories remain readable after you stop subscribing.
