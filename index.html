How it works

Talk, repair, stay close

Three jobs, and the app opens on the one you need today.

💬

Something to talk about

Over 4,000 questions across nine kinds of conversation, from light to genuinely deep. Bondify picks one that suits the person and the moment, so you're never scrolling a menu looking for a prompt.

🛠️

When something is wrong

23 guided plans for the things that actually go wrong between people. A few minutes a day, one small step at a time. Nothing is timed, nothing is locked behind a streak — miss a day and pick it up when you can.

📷

The part you keep

Memories with the story behind them, kept per person. Bondify hands one back to you years later, on the morning it happened.

🎁

Wishlist

Note what they mention wanting, the moment they mention it. It's waiting when their birthday comes round.

📅

Together since

The day it started, counted forward. Anniversaries and milestones arrive before you've missed them.

📱

On your Home Screen

Widgets for how long you've been together, the next birthday or anniversary, and a photo from your memories that changes each day. Lock Screen complications too.
